She wears an elegant cozy cream knit cardigan over a modest dusty-rose cottagecore dress with tiny embroidered flowers, delicate pendant, soft long sleeves. Her hands gently hold a small cream ceramic tea cup at waist height, anatomically correct hands. Polished high-quality hand-drawn anime visual novel illustration, subtle painterly shading, refined thin linework, soft warm light and gorgeous fabric/hair detail. Front-facing portrait from top of head down to mid-thigh, centered composition, no cropping head/hair edges; hair framing her silhouette, character fills 90 percent of height. Actual transparent background with clean alpha edges, no scenery, no floor, no oval frame, no other characters, no text, no logos, no watermark, no sparkles outside silhouette. Friendly, calming, romantic pastel aesthetic.
